The rules of the crypt

Break them and the pumpkins will know.

  1. Inspect every pumpkin

    Tap any pumpkin to see it up close. Look at all 21 before you decide. The patch keeps count of how many you've seen.

  2. Choose your top three

    Pick your 1st, 2nd and 3rd favourite. Three different pumpkins.

  3. Points are awarded

    Your 1st choice gets 3 points, 2nd gets 2 points and 3rd gets 1 point.

  4. One ballot per soul

    Sign it with your name. Vote again with the same name and your new ballot replaces the old one.

  5. No voting for your own

    Don't put your own pumpkin in your top three. It's the honour system, and the spirits are watching.

  6. Closes Thursday 5 November

    Voting ends at midnight (23:59 UK time) on Thursday 5 November. Votes stay secret until then.

  7. The winners

    Most points wins 1st, 2nd and 3rd place. A tie goes to the pumpkin with more 1st-place votes, then more 2nd-place votes.
